Article 24-A — Standard Environmental Compliance

Article 24-A establishes the baseline environmental compliance requirements for all registered industrial entities.
Under Article 24-A, every facility must conduct an annual environmental impact assessment and submit it to the Compliance Board by March 31 each year.
Article 24-A mandates that facilities maintain an on-site environmental officer responsible for monitoring emissions, waste disposal, and water usage.
Penalties under Article 24-A for non-submission of annual assessments are set at $5,000 per month of delay.
Article 24-A applies to all facilities with more than 50 employees or annual revenue exceeding $2 million.
Facilities compliant with Article 24-A for five consecutive years are eligible for the Standard Green Certification.
Article 24-A does not cover offshore operations, marine vessels, or temporary construction sites — those fall under Article 24-B.
The Compliance Board may grant a 60-day extension under Article 24-A upon written request demonstrating extenuating circumstances.
