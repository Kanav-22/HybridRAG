Climate Policy 2024 - Amendment and Updates

The Climate Policy Amendment of 2024 revised the emission cap from 500 tonnes to 400 tonnes annually.
Fines for excess emissions increased to $350 per excess tonne under the 2024 revision.
New in 2024: offshore manufacturing units are now included in the emission registry requirement.
The quarterly reporting deadline was changed to the 10th of each quarter month under the 2024 amendment.
Section 14-B was added in 2024, extending the green technology rebate to include solar infrastructure.
The 2024 policy introduced mandatory third-party audits for factories exceeding 1000 tonnes annually.
Companies with zero excess emissions for 3 consecutive years qualify for a Gold Compliance Certificate.
