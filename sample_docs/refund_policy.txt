Customer Refund and Return Policy

Customers may return products within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
Items must be in original packaging and unused condition to qualify for a refund.
Digital products and software licenses are non-refundable once activated.
Refund requests must be submitted through the customer portal at support.example.com.
Processing time for approved refunds is 5-7 business days to the original payment method.
Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a product defect.
For defective items, customers are entitled to a full refund including original shipping costs.
Exchanges are permitted within 60 days of purchase for items of equal or lesser value.
Bulk orders of 10 units or more are subject to a restocking fee of 15%.
