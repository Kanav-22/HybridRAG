Climate Policy 2023 - Annual Report

The Climate Policy Act of 2023 introduced carbon emission caps for industrial manufacturers.
Factories emitting more than 500 tonnes of CO2 annually must register with the National Emission Registry.
Companies that exceed their emission quota face a fine of $200 per excess tonne.
The 2023 policy introduced a cap-and-trade mechanism allowing companies to buy and sell emission credits.
All registered factories must submit quarterly emission reports by the 15th of January, April, July, and October.
The Climate Board oversees enforcement and has authority to conduct surprise audits.
Green technology investments qualify for a 30% tax rebate under Section 14-A of the 2023 Act.
